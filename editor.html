Lick Chords

Patches 28 (WAYLO_LICK_CHORD_1) and 29 (WAYLO_LICK_CHORD_2) play chords whose notes move through a melody ("lick"). Playing a note at C4 or above selects a chord position (C4=0, C#4=1, D4=2 …). Each voice has a chord note (actual MIDI note with octave) and a starting position in the melody. At position 0 each voice plays its chord note; as position advances the voice follows the melody, transposed by chordNote − melody[startPos] to stay in its octave. Notes below C4 silently set transposition (same as CHORD_SCALE patches).

MIDI import format: Bar 1 whole notes = actual chord notes (with octave). Quarter notes = melody/lick in order. Half notes = starting position per voice, encoded as note % 12 (C=0, C#=1, D=2, D#=3, E=4, F=5 …).
