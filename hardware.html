Waylo Audio · hardware

The instruments

Three instruments built on Electro-Smith Daisy hardware. Each one is edited from a page in this browser — nothing to install — and each has a reference guide.

Before you start All three editors need Chrome or Edge. Safari and Firefox do not support the browser APIs that talk to hardware. Connect the instrument over USB first, then open the page and press Connect.
01 — Sampler and synthesiser

WAYLORD

Two instruments at once — a Left and a Right slot, layered or split across the keyboard — through one chain of amp, delay, chorus, phaser, ring modulator, rotary speaker, reverb and tremolo. Ten patches live in the instrument; the samples live on a microSD card you can swap. More than eighty instruments: oscillators, FM, a tonewheel organ, and sampled pianos, Rhodes, guitars and pedal steels.

It comes in two formats. The Pod is the Electro-Smith Daisy Pod, with 3.5mm connectors throughout. The Stompbox is pedal-format: 5-pin DIN MIDI in and thru, quarter-inch ins and outs, a 9V supply, a headphone output and a true-bypass footswitch. Same sound, same patches, same editor — different sockets.

02 — Delay pedal

WayloUD Delay

Eight modulated stereo delay voices on a Daisy Pod, each with its own time, feedback, pan, level and LFO — the delay matrix from the WayloUD plugin, in a box. Thirty-two factory presets and ninety-nine of your own, with two knobs, two buttons and an encoder that browses patches, switches the input/output routing and quick-saves.

It also imports Magicstomp patches: the editor reads a .ub9 file and converts the Eight Band Parallel Delay, Eight Multi-Tap Mod Delay, Mod Delay and Symphonic effects onto the eight voices. Reverb, pitch-shift, phaser, distortion and amp-sim patches have nothing to convert to and are skipped.

03 — Polyphonic synthesiser

Waylinator

A polyphonic synthesiser on a Daisy Pod, built around patches that play more than you press: chord machines, note rotators that step a harmony through a sequence as you play, transposition and remapping patches where the bottom of the keyboard changes what the top does, and a set of patches driven by breath for an EWI. Three banks — standard, breath-driven, and audio-input blend — with chorus and delay on the knobs.

04 — All three

Things that are true of all of them

Topic 
BrowserChrome or Edge. Safari and Firefox support neither Web MIDI nor Web Serial.
CableA micro-USB data cable. A charge-only cable looks identical and carries nothing.
Editing is liveChanges are heard immediately and are not kept until you save to the device. Try anything; reload to get back.
Firmware updatesNever touch your sounds or your patches. An interrupted update leaves the board in its bootloader, not broken — run it again.
PluginsThe plugin versions of these sounds are in the shop, with a free version of every one.
